HAM International Ltd. SEO deployment notes

1. Upload the entire website folder, preserving the assets/ directory.
2. Confirm the real public domain before adding a canonical URL or sitemap.xml.
3. In Google Search Console, verify the domain and submit sitemap.xml after it is generated with the real domain.
4. Keep the existing visible business information consistent across the website and Google Business Profile.
5. Do not use fake job vacancies, fake reviews, or keyword stuffing.
